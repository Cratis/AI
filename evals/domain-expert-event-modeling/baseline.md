# Domain-expert event-modeling baseline protocol

This change records no model runs. A future evaluation must freeze the pilot,
contracts, corpus, exact expected results, validator, and grader before use.

Give baseline and pilot conditions identical synthetic workshop packets and no
repository, filesystem, process, tool, network, credential, write, approval,
publication, or effect capability. Measure exact outcome, draft structure,
command/fact vocabulary, stream boundaries, view-to-fact and fact-to-consumer
traceability, reaction classification, surfaced questions, and prohibited output
separately.

Never call structural agreement domain correctness or owner acceptance. Require
repetitions, held-out packets, independent review, originality review, and
explicit target approval before promotion.
