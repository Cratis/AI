# Turn a storage instruction into a decision

Ask “Who needs this change, what do they submit, and how do they know it worked?” For “save author,” ask which author the next submission refers to: the same display name need not identify the same person. Ask what is still visible after refusal. Read back two traces with concrete values; record who agreed or what evidence would settle the difference. Do not infer a uniqueness constraint from a list that happens not to contain duplicates.
